ASH BAND — Screenless AI Wristband

Screenless Spatial Audio & Gesture Hardware Interface

🌐 Web Endpoints: 🔗 ash-band.web.app 🔗 ashband.netlify.app

ASH BAND redefines screenless physical computing. Designed as a premium AI wristband, it replaces visual screen clutter with continuous voice intelligence and micro-gesture motion controls. Powered by an integrated gyroscope and real-time audio pipeline, users interact using subtle spatial hand gestures without looking at a display.

Core Hardware Features

🤏 Double-Pinch Gesture

Gyroscope tracking registers quick thumb + index double-pinches to trigger, interrupt, or pause AI voice interactions on the fly.

🎛️ Rotational Motion Controls

Natural wrist rotation gestures enable smooth volume adjustments, voice mode toggles, and item scrolling.

🤖 Remote Tech Orchestration

Direct connectivity with integrated AI agents allows remote control over smart devices and connected desktop tech.

🎙️ Dynamic Voice Personas

Seamlessly switch between predefined AI voice personas or custom synthesized user profiles on demand.
