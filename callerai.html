📞 Voice Simulation Web App

CallerAI — Simulated Voice Environment

Proactive & Inbound Simulated Telephony

🌐 Web Endpoint: 🔗 callyourai.netlify.app

CallerAI simulates phone interactions by creating custom AI contacts with tailored behavioral scripts, dialpads, and call scheduling. Blending speech synthesis with contextual ambient sound layers, it delivers realistic voice call simulations.

System Architecture

📱 Contact & Dialpad Builder

Define virtual numbers, avatars, and custom prompt rules for tailored phone contacts.

⏰ Proactive & Scheduled Inbound

Configure automated call times for AI contacts to initiate incoming calls directly to you.

🎧 Ambient Audio Engine

Inject real background sound clips (construction sites, rain, coffee shops) directly into the call stream.

🛍️ AI Bazar Hub

Share and clone community-built caller personas and custom setup configurations.
